## Key points to the new SEO

- Content is the new cool — identify your audience and write content nobody else has
- Find what your customers are trying to solve, and offer solutions before they finish asking
- Share your posts and pages where your audience already spends time

## Content — more natural and holistic

Search engines have gotten much better at reading intent, not just keywords. That means the old game of stuffing a page with a target phrase does less every year. What moves rankings now is content built around a real question, written the way a person would actually explain it — structured, specific, and useful enough that people stay, link to it, and come back.

The studios that win at search today treat it less like a technical checklist and more like editorial: know who you're writing for, know what they're stuck on, and answer it better than anyone else has.
