## Java and the coffee cup

Have you ever wondered why the Java programming language is called Java? The name refers to a cup of coffee — a nod to the culture the language was born into, and one that never really left the craft.

## The coffee culture

Having a cup of coffee within reach while writing complicated code has become a common habit among programmers. It's almost a trigger for finding solutions to complicated problems. Sometimes it feels like ideas are tied to that cup, because in many cases creativity flows after one or two sips.

## Caffeine adds up

There is research proving that caffeine has cognitive benefits — that it "makes you smarter." When you have coffee, the caffeine binds to the same receptors your body would otherwise use for adenosine, the compound that makes you feel tired. Fewer signals telling you to slow down, more focus on the problem in front of you.

Code without coffee? That's completely wrong at every level of society — about as wrong as designing a logo without Illustrator.
