## Context

An early-stage fintech had strong acquisition but weak activation. Users downloaded the app, started identity verification, then stalled when the flow felt opaque or brittle on mid-range Android devices.

## Problem

KYC asked for too much, too early, with little explanation of why each step mattered. Error states were generic. Support volume grew while conversion stayed flat.

## Approach

We remapped onboarding around progressive trust: explain, collect, confirm. UI patterns surfaced progress, recovery paths, and document guidance with real device constraints in mind. Engineering shipped a resilient mobile client with offline-tolerant uploads and clearer API error mapping.

## Result

KYC completion improved 41%. Support tickets related to onboarding dropped 22%. Time from signup to first funded account fell 18%.

> “We stopped guessing where people quit — the product now tells them what to do next.”
