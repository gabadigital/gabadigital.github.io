## Context

A last-mile operator running routes across Lagos and Accra was coordinating drivers over phone calls and a shared spreadsheet. Every reroute meant a dispatcher manually calling drivers one at a time, and customers had no way to know when a delivery was actually coming.

## Problem

Dispatchers could not see where drivers were or which stops were behind schedule until a customer called to complain. Route changes took minutes to communicate instead of seconds. Failed deliveries were retried blind, without knowing what had gone wrong on the first attempt.

## Approach

We built a dispatcher dashboard with live route status, so a controller could see every driver's progress against schedule at a glance and reroute with a couple of taps instead of a phone tree. On the driver side, we shipped a lightweight mobile app with a step-by-step delivery checklist, proof-of-delivery capture, and one-tap exception reporting that fed straight back into the dashboard.

## Result

On-time delivery rose 24%. Dispatchers cut average reroute time by 31%, and failed deliveries dropped 17% now that drivers could log the real reason on the spot instead of a generic "not delivered."

> "We finally know where every delivery is before the customer has to tell us."
