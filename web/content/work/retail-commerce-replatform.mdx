## Context

A growing retailer selling across UAE and West Africa was stuck on a slow theme stack. Product pages loaded inconsistently on mobile, and merchandising teams could not ship campaigns without developer bottlenecks.

## Problem

Shoppers abandoned carts on patchy networks. Category navigation buried bestsellers. Performance debt made paid traffic expensive: ads sent people into a store that took too long to become useful.

## Approach

We ran a conversion-focused UX audit, then rebuilt the storefront around three jobs: find, compare, buy. The new system used a componentized product detail template, edge-friendly image delivery, and a streamlined checkout with clearer shipping and payment states. Merchandising received a content model that made campaign landing pages shippable without redeploys.

## Result

Checkout completion rose 28%. Mobile LCP landed under 2.4 seconds on key category pages. Merchandising launched seasonal campaigns in days instead of weeks.

> “The store finally feels as fast as the ads we run against it.”
